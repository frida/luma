QEMU
====

This directory contains QEMU 11.1.0, an independent program published
by the QEMU project under the GNU General Public License, version 2.
Its full licence terms are in COPYING and COPYING.LIB beside this file.

Luma itself is MIT-licensed and does not link against QEMU. It starts
the emulators here as separate processes and speaks to them over QMP
and D-Bus, so the two remain separate works, merely distributed
together.

The binaries are the unmodified Windows build published at

    https://qemu.weilnetz.de/w64/qemu-w64-setup-20260811.exe

and the corresponding source for QEMU 11.1.0 is available from

    https://download.qemu.org/qemu-11.1.0.tar.xz
    https://gitlab.com/qemu-project/qemu

For three years from the date this copy was distributed, the Luma
project will on request supply the complete corresponding source of
these binaries on a physical medium, for no more than the cost of
performing that distribution. Write to https://github.com/frida/luma.
